Backend Systems: The Living Engine

The backend of KILL THE BILL is built on a highly reactive, interconnected systemic web. Every action ripples through the economy, social fabric, and local sector dynamics.

The Systemic Loop Schema

graph TD A[Player Action] -->|Assassinate, Hack, Sabotage| B(WorldDirector) B -->|Adjusts Global Wealth & Tension| C{Global Economy} C -->|Food Price, Security Level| D[Sector Dynamics] C -->|Dictates Zeitgeist| E[Social Fabric Engine] D -->|Changes Local Modifiers| F[Active Map Constraints] E -->|Calculates Cohesion vs Defiance| G[NPC Controller] F -->|Spawns Hazards & Guards| G G -->|Injects Context Payload| H((LLM API)) H -->|Generates Dialogue & Action| I[NPC Reacts to Player]

Core System Components

Singleton WorldDirector

The central brain. Manages the wealth, paranoia, and survival of the 4 Oligarchs. It calculates the global economy variables whenever an event is triggered.

  • Food Price: Drives NPC survival needs.
  • Security Level: Dictates Enforcer spawn rates.
  • Senate Alignment: Corporate vs. Reformist control.

Mechanic Social Fabric Engine

An Epidemic Influence Model calculating crowd psychology. Driven by the Sector's Zeitgeist (average mood).

  • Cohesive NPCs: Drift toward the crowd's mood.
  • Defiant NPCs: Rebel against the crowd's mood.

Integration LLM Context Payload

NPCs do not have scripted trees. When the player talks to an NPC, the NPCController gathers the NPC's social class, mood, and global economic constraints to form a JSON payload for the LLM.
