# Pitch

**KILL THE BILL** is a 3D isometric immersive sim where the player is a nameless citizen in a late-stage capitalist dystopia, and the goal is simple: take down the billionaires.

Every run, the game builds a new world. A procedural map of 6–10 regions. A cast of 4–6 billionaires with their own personalities, ambitions, and rivalries. A street-level population of 40 persistent citizens who remember what you did. A live news feed — the **NetFeed** — narrating the world's reaction in real time.

You choose your approach. Stealth and assassination. Economic sabotage that bankrupts the oligarchy from the inside. Political organizing that walks a revolution into the Enclave. Or any mix — the three paths are tracked at once and interact in ugly, interesting ways.

Nothing is scripted. The story emerges from systems: a greedy Media Oligarch buys his rival's newspaper, and a scandal you planted suddenly has no outlet. A paranoid Food Oligarch raises grain prices and your Worker neighbor radicalizes three cycles faster than you expected. A stranger shakes your hand in a back-alley bar and two hours later your entire playthrough is a chaos cascade called Project Mayhem.

The world doesn't wait for the player. It evolves on its own — through LLM-generated events, billionaire decisions, NPC psychology — and the player's job is to read the cracks and drive a wedge through them before the system crushes them.

Die and you come back as someone else. The billionaires don't reset. The consequences don't reset. The world remembers even when you don't.
