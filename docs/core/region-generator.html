RegionGenerator

The procedural world map builder. At playthrough start, generates 6-10 unique regions with random names, properties, and visual biomes. No two playthroughs have the same geography. Replaces all hardcoded sector names.

Region Types

URBAN_SLUM 2-3 Generated

Dense brutalist slums. High tension, low security. Names like "Rust Hollow", "Ash Sprawl", "Smog Warren".

Targets: Water Treatment, Power Relay, Comm Array, Food Depot, Clinic

Biomes: brutalist_fog, neon_rain, concrete_decay, toxic_sprawl

URBAN_ELITE 1-2 Generated

Pristine corporate plazas. Low tension, high security. Names like "Crystal Heights", "Ivory Pinnacle".

Targets: Financial Server, Senate Hall, Luxury Mall, Private Hangar

Biomes: glass_spire, garden_terrace, white_brutalism, floating_plaza

INDUSTRIAL 1-2 Generated

Factories and refineries. Medium tension. Names like "Foundry Basin", "Slag Works".

Targets: Refinery, Assembly Line, Waste Processor, Mining Shaft, Rail Depot

Biomes: smoke_stack, rust_canyon, molten_core, warehouse_grid

AGRICULTURAL 1-2 Generated

Underground farms and food production. Low population. Names like "Substrate Fields", "Root Vaults".

Targets: Hydroponic Vault, Grain Silo, Livestock Dome, Seed Bank, Irrigation Hub

Biomes: underground_green, terraced_cavern, dome_farm, strip_field

ISLAND_RETREAT 0-1 Generated

Maximum security Oligarch compounds. Names like "Haven Cay", "Obsidian Atoll".

Targets: Private Dock, Oligarch Villa, Escape Vessel, Comm Jammer

Biomes: tropical_fortress, volcanic_bunker, arctic_retreat, ocean_platform

TRANSIT 1-2 Generated

Border zones and checkpoints. Smuggling opportunities. Names like "Checkpoint Nexus", "Passage Lock".

Targets: Checkpoint Scanner, Cargo Bay, Smuggler Tunnel, ID Forge

Biomes: highway_corridor, rail_junction, border_wall, underground_passage

Name Generation

Each region type has a pool of 10 prefixes and 10 suffixes. At generation, all pools are shuffled and names are composed as prefix + " " + suffix. This produces ~100 unique names per type, ensuring no two playthroughs share the same geography.

Region Properties

PropertyTypeDescription
security_modifierintAdded to global security_presence for local checks
tension_modifierintAdded to global public_tension for local NPC behavior
population_densityfloat (0-1)Affects NPC encounter frequency and crowd sizes
