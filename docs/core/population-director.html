PopulationDirector Singleton

The PopulationDirector manages the persistent NPC roster. At the start of each playthrough, it generates a fixed cast of ~40 unique characters with fully randomized intrinsic personality traits. These NPCs persist for the entire run and evolve based on world pressure filtered through their immutable nature. No NPC is spawned or despawned mid-game.

Design Philosophy

NPCs are not disposable. They are persistent characters the player can form relationships with, recruit as agents, or watch evolve into radicals or broken shells depending on the systemic pressures. Each playthrough generates a completely different roster, ensuring high replayability.

Roster Generation (generate_new_roster())

Called once at the start of a new playthrough. The function creates MAX_NPCS (40) persistent NPCData resources.

Step 1: Name Generation

Two pools of 40 first names and 40 last names are shuffled. Names are drawn sequentially using modulo indexing to ensure uniqueness within a run. The pools are multicultural to reflect the collapsed global society.

Step 2: Social Class Assignment

Each NPC is assigned a class via weighted random roll:

Class Weight Rationale
Oligarch 5% Extremely rare. High starting wealth (800-1500cr).
Enforcer 10% The system's enforcers. Moderate wealth (150-400cr).
Worker 40% The struggling majority. Low wealth (30-120cr).
Destitute 45% The bottom. Near-zero wealth (0-30cr).

Step 3: Intrinsic Trait Randomization

All 6 intrinsic traits (resilience, aggression, empathy, idealism, greed, conformity) are randomized across the full 0.0-1.0 range using randf(). After randomization, slight class-based biases are applied:

  • Oligarchs: greed += 0.2, empathy -= 0.2 (upbringing favors self-interest)
  • Enforcers: conformity += 0.2, aggression += 0.1 (military conditioning)
  • Destitute: resilience += 0.1 (survival of the toughest)

Step 4: Quirk Assignment

Each NPC receives 1-3 random quirks drawn from a shuffled combined pool of ~56 quirks across 5 categories:

Category Count Examples
Habits 12 "Always chewing something", "Fidgets with a broken lighter"
Speech 12 "Stutters when nervous", "Swears constantly"
Physical 12 "Has a limp from an Enforcer beating", "One eye clouded over"
Fears 10 "Terrified of enclosed spaces", "Flinches at loud noises"
Backstory 12 "Lost a child to water rationing", "Former Enforcer who deserted"

Quirks are injected into the LLM prompt as "Quirks you MUST embody: ..." to ensure every conversation feels human.

World Cycle Evolution (evolve_all_npcs())

Called by the WorldDirector each game cycle. Iterates through the entire roster and passes the current global_economy dictionary to each NPC's process_world_pressure() method. The same raw numbers produce different outcomes per NPC because of their unique intrinsic trait filters.

Parameters passed: tension, food_price, security_presence

Query Functions

get_npc_by_id(id)

Returns a specific NPC by their unique npc_id string. Used for targeted interactions.

get_radicals()

Returns an array of all NPCs whose get_behavioral_profile() is currently "Radical Agitator". Useful for the player to find potential violent allies.

get_broken()

Returns all NPCs profiled as "Broken and Submissive". These are potential exploitation targets or indicators of extreme oppression.

get_population_summary()

Returns a Dictionary mapping behavioral profiles to their count. E.g., {"Radical Agitator": 3, "Anxious Citizen": 22, ...}. Powers the UI population mood indicator.
