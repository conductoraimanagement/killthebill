Player Manager Singleton

The PlayerManager tracks the player's active archetype, chosen characteristics, and persistent state. It dictates starting conditions, crisis events, and how the world perceives the player.

Player Archetypes (Class Seeds)

ARCHETYPE White Collar

The Disgraced Elite. Starts with high Intel (passwords, map data) but extremely low Social Capital (hated by the masses).

Crisis Event: Hunted by the "Compliance AI" which tracks digital footprints and hacks.

ARCHETYPE Blue Collar

The Discarded Laborer. Starts with low Intel but high Social Capital (invisibility to locals, access to black markets).

Crisis Event: "The Resource Squeeze" - A constant drain on credits needed to buy clean air and water.

Generic Characteristics (Traits)

TRAIT Corporate Face

High Charisma/Manipulation. Bribing Security Oligarchs costs 50% less. White Collar NPCs start with higher baseline affinity towards you.

TRAIT Ghost in the Machine

High Tech Savvy. Hacking the grid causes massive tension spikes but generates less "Paranoia" for the Security Oligarch. Unlocks unique tech dialogue options.

TRAIT Street Agitator

High Intimidation/Populism. Exerting Political Pressure against the Enclave is twice as effective. Merely entering a Sector permanently raises its baseline Tension by +10.
