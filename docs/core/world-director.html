World Director Singleton

The WorldDirector is the central brain of the simulation. It manages the global economy, tracks the status of the 4 Oligarchs, and calculates the systemic "Butterfly Effect" ripples that occur after any major player action.

Global State Variables

ECONOMY Resource Prices

Food Price (Base: 100cr): Drives the survival needs of Blue Collar NPCs. Sabotaging farms causes this to spike.

Tech Price (Base: 500cr): Determines the cost of hacking tools and black market weapons.

SOCIETY Public Tension

Tension Level (0-100): How close the Sinks are to a full-scale riot. Increased by poverty and police brutality.

Senate Alignment: Ranges from Corporate (-100) to Reformist (100). Influenced by player propaganda.

SECURITY Paranoia Level

Security Presence (0-100): High values increase Enforcer spawn rates and checkpoint lockdowns. Spikes when an Oligarch is assassinated.

PR & Reputation Engine (The Narrative Layer)

To ensure the world feels deep and reactive, the WorldDirector continuously tracks the Public Image of all major actors (Oligarchs, Politicians, Unions). This is fueled by a daily procedural News Cycle where actors generate scandals, quotes, and decisions.

ACTOR STATE Public Image & Controversy

  • Public Image (-100 to 100): How loved or hated the Oligarch is by the masses.
  • Controversy Level (0 to 100): How actively the public is discussing them. High controversy acts as a multiplier for public tension.
  • Scandals Array: A rolling ledger of recent quotes or actions (e.g., "Caught hoarding synthetic meat").

NEWS CYCLE Procedural Gaffes

The LLM periodically generates "Quotes" for Oligarchs based on their current paranoia and wealth. For example, if an Oligarch says, "I'm not sure humanity should endure," their Public Image plummets and Sector Zeitgeist shifts violently against them.

MANIPULATION Media Hacking

Players can weaponize the PR system. By hacking the Media Conglomerate, players can artificially inject fake scandals or leak real ones, destroying an Oligarch's reputation before firing a single bullet.

Expanded Butterfly Effect Matrix

EVENT Out-of-Touch Quote

"Humanity shouldn't endure."

  • Immediate: Tech Oligarch's Public Image crashes to -90. Controversy spikes.
  • Systemic: Enclave stock prices crash. Sinks NPCs aggressively target Tech infrastructure for vandalism.

ACTION Assassinate Media Mogul

The propaganda machine stops.

  • Immediate: Controversy Level for ALL surviving Oligarchs spikes to 100.
  • Systemic: Without propaganda shielding them, every minor Oligarch scandal now causes immediate, violent riots in The Sinks.

ACTION Leak Food Hoarding Scandal

Player uploads Datashards to the net.

  • Immediate: Food Oligarch Public Image drops. Senate approval drops.
  • Systemic: Food prices actually drop temporarily as the Oligarch tries to buy back public favor, but Security patrols double to protect the silos.
