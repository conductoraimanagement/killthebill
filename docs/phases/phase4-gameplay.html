PHASE 4 Direct Action Gameplay

The final phase implements the lethal, physical consequences of the game. It introduces combat, noise mechanics, security escalation (Heat), and the roguelite succession cycle.

STEP 4.1 Combat Raycasting

Implementation of weapon mechanics.

  • Mechanics: Physics raycasting from the player to the mouse position.
  • Damage Models: Implementing health variables for NPCs and physical infrastructure (e.g., blowing up servers).
  • Noise Generation: Classifying weapons (Silent Crossbows vs. Loud Automatics) which trigger varied audio spheres that alert nearby NPCs.

STEP 4.2 The Heat System

Security escalation and AI hunting.

  • Crime Events: A signal bus triggered when cameras or loyalist NPCs witness violence/sabotage.
  • Tier Spawning: Depending on the Local Heat level, dynamically instantiating Police (Tier 1) or Enforcer Hitsquads (Tier 2) at the edge of the navmesh.
  • Hunter AI: Writing the behavior tree for Enforcers to track the player's last known position using the NavigationAgent3D.

STEP 4.3 Roguelite Loop (Succession)

Handling death and consequence persistence.

  • DeathScreen UI: A dedicated Control scene triggered on player health = 0 or arrest.
  • Data Persistence: Saving the WorldDirector global economy dictionary to disk (JSON) to ensure Butterfly Effects persist across runs.
  • Respawn: Passing the player back to the PlayerManager menu to generate a new Class Seed citizen in the scarred world.
