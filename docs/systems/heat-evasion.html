The Heat System: Crime & Evasion

Doing something illegal (firing automatics, placing bombs, assaulting an Oligarch) generates a CrimeEvent. If you are caught on camera or spotted by a loyalist NPC, your Local Heat Level instantly spikes, triggering an escalating response from the Security Oligarch.

Escalation Tiers

TIER 1 Local Police

Beat cops and corporate security are dispatched to your last known location. They will investigate the area, question NPCs, and attempt to arrest you.

TIER 2 SWAT / Enforcers

Heavily armored corporate hit squads deploy via dropships. They use lethal force immediately and lock down Sector exits.

TIER 3 Martial Law

Military mechs or the Hunter AI are deployed. The entire Region goes into lockdown, and travel is completely frozen.

Evasion Mechanics

You cannot simply outrun a Tier 2 or 3 response. To successfully evade capture and reset your Heat Level, you must:

  • Break Line of Sight: Hide in designated safe zones, like smuggler tunnels or off-grid safehouses.
  • Change Identifiers: Swap outfits or use black-market biometric scrubbers to break visual tracking.
  • Hack or Wait: Wait out the cooldown timer (which drains much slower if the global_security_presence is high). Alternatively, hack a regional security server to manually wipe your wanted status.
