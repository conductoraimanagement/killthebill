Macro-World & Procedural Generation

The game world is not a single city, but a sprawling geographical area containing multiple Regions, each with distinct visual styles and economic functions. Travel between these regions carries significant risk and forms a core pillar of the Immersive Sim experience.

The Region Map (Macro Scale)

URBAN The Mega-Cities

Dense urban centers divided strictly between The Sinks (low-altitude brutalist slums) and The Enclave (high-altitude pristine corporate plazas). High population density and heavily policed.

INDUSTRIAL The Countryside

Subterranean farms, strip mines, and automated manufacturing zones. Low population density, but crucial for sabotaging the global economy. Wide open spaces make stealth difficult.

ISOLATED The Islands

Highly isolated, heavily guarded private zones where the top 0.1% live. Reaching an Island Retreat is usually the final step in a Direct Action assassination run.

Active Travel Risks

Moving between Regions is not a simple loading screen. Players must bypass Border Checkpoints. If your Heat Level is high, or if you lack the proper corporate credentials, you must utilize your Intel, credits (bribes), or physical stealth (stowing away on cargo trains) to physically cross into the next map.
