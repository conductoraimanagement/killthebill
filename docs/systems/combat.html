Combat & Weaponry (Direct Action)

While social manipulation is powerful, the game fully supports lethal "Direct Action." The combat system utilizes 3D isometric twin-stick or tactical point-and-click targeting. Weapons are highly systemic and interact directly with the game's economy and stealth mechanics.

Weapon Categories & Systemic Impacts

STEALTH Sniper Rifles & Crossbows

Mechanics: Require line-of-sight and charge time. Crossbows are completely silent; snipers are loud but penetrate Enclave armor.

Impact: Silent kills prevent Sector Tension from spiking immediately, allowing for clean assassinations.

SABOTAGE Bombs & Traps

Mechanics: Proximity mines, remote explosives, and EMP traps. Can be placed on patrol routes or critical infrastructure.

Impact: Blowing up a target with a bomb triggers a massive Butterfly Effect (e.g., skyrocketing prices) and instantly shifts the Sector Zeitgeist into panic or riot mode.

ASSAULT Guns & Automatics

Mechanics: High DPS, wide cone of fire, but extremely loud.

Impact: Firing an automatic weapon immediately flags the player, spiking the security_presence and causing civilian NPCs to scatter. Ammo is a scarce resource tied to the Tech economy.

The Black Market Economy

The availability and price of these weapons on the Black Market fluctuate based on the WorldDirector. If the Security Oligarch is highly paranoid, gun prices skyrocket, making crafted traps or a silent crossbow much more viable.
