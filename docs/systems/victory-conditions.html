Victory Conditions

The game does not have a linear ending. Instead, the WorldDirector continuously monitors three Collapse Meters. Reaching critical mass in any meter triggers a unique endgame sequence. The player can pursue multiple paths simultaneously or commit fully to one.

The Three Paths

PATH 1 Direct Action (Assassination)

Objective: Physically terminate 3-5 procedurally generated Billionaires.

Mechanics:

  • Each Oligarch has a unique location, security detail, and daily routine that shifts based on their paranoia level.
  • Killing an Oligarch triggers massive systemic ripples: their sector of the economy destabilizes, security spikes globally, and surviving Oligarchs increase paranoia.
  • The player must plan escape routes before engaging — the Heat system will escalate to Tier 3 (Military/Compliance AI) after an assassination.

Endgame:

Power vacuum. The system collapses violently. Heaviest combat ending. The Sinks erupt into total chaos as the economic pillars evaporate.

PATH 2 Systemic Collapse (Economic Hacking)

Objective: Manipulate the Butterfly Effect to bankrupt The Enclave. Drive all Oligarch wealth to zero.

Mechanics:

  • Sabotage food supply chains to spike prices → riots → security costs increase → Oligarch wealth drains.
  • Hack financial systems to redirect funds.
  • Leak scandals via NetFeed manipulation to crash Oligarch public image → stock prices tank.
  • Exert political pressure to shift Senate alignment away from corporate interests.

Endgame:

The Enclave loses its ability to pay Enforcers. The security grid shuts down. A quiet, corporate death. The system doesn't explode — it simply stops functioning.

PATH 3 Political Revolution (NPC Persuasion)

Objective: Maximize Social Capital in The Sinks. Manipulate the NPC roster into forming a unified faction. Push the global public_tension to critical mass (100).

Mechanics:

  • Build relationships with key NPCs across the roster — recruit Revolutionary Idealists as leaders.
  • Use the Contextual Persuasion UI to incite groups, spread propaganda, and organize resistance.
  • The Social Fabric Engine must show a critical mass of radicalized or revolutionary NPCs.
  • The player must physically escort/protect the mob during the final march on The Enclave.

Endgame:

The masses storm The Enclave. The player wins by successfully escorting the mob to the core citadel/server. The revolution succeeds — but at what cost to the NPCs the player bonded with?

Hybrid Approaches

The three paths are not mutually exclusive. A player might assassinate the Media Mogul (Path 1) to remove propaganda shielding, then leverage the resulting chaos to radicalize NPCs (Path 3), while simultaneously hacking financial systems (Path 2). The WorldDirector tracks all three collapse meters in parallel, and the first to hit critical mass determines the endgame sequence.

Roguelite Persistence

If the player dies or is arrested before triggering any victory condition, the game does not end. Time skips forward. The player spawns as a new citizen with a new Class Seed. However, the WorldDirector state persists — all economic damage, NPC evolution, and Oligarch deaths carry over. The new character inherits the consequences of the old one.
