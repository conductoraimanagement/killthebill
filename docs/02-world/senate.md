# The Senate

> **Status:** Design spec + stub. See [src/core/SenateDirector.gd](../../src/core/SenateDirector.gd).

The Senate is the game's **third power lever**, alongside direct action (combat, sabotage) and information warfare (NetFeed, scandals). Eleven [politicians](../03-characters/politicians.md) sit in it. Every world cycle, a **Bill** enters the docket — procedurally generated by the LLM from current world pressures — and the Senate votes. The outcome mutates the economy, triggers NetFeed coverage, and gives oligarchs something to lobby against or ride.

No bill is scripted. The list of possible bills *is the world state itself, filtered through a sponsor*.

---

## Design goals

1. **The Senate must feel responsive.** A scandal last cycle should produce an investigation bill this cycle — not a random tax policy. The politicians *read the room* before they legislate.
2. **The LLM writes the bill; the sim enforces the bill.** Titles, summaries, rhetoric, and framing are LLM. Mechanical effects on the economy are constrained to a schema so the sim stays coherent.
3. **Bribes buy votes, not outcomes.** A bill can pass despite oligarch opposition if the public is angry enough. Money is a strong lever, never a monopoly.
4. **Reading the docket should be fun.** Players will screenshot good bill titles. Framing matters — *"The Public Safety and Decency Act"* is more interesting than *"+15 security."*
5. **The player can participate without being a politician.** The whole point is *outside* influence — lobbying, bribing, blackmailing, leaking, assassinating, or just letting the world vote on itself.

---

## The Senate cycle

```
Every world cycle:
  1. SenateDirector._pick_sponsor()          # weighted by faction pressure
  2. SenateDirector._propose_bill_async()    # LLM generates bill from context
  3. SenateDirector._open_debate()           # player-interaction window (1 cycle)
  4. SenateDirector._tally_votes()           # politicians vote in charisma order
  5. WorldDirector._apply_bill_effects()     # economy mutates
  6. NetFeed triggered with result + quotes
  7. Oligarch patronage ledger updated
```

Each bill lives exactly one cycle in debate. Rejected bills do not return verbatim — but a similar bill may be proposed later, generated fresh from updated world state.

## Bill schema

The LLM is required to produce this exact JSON. Effects may only touch the **whitelisted variables** (the 5 global economy vars, region modifiers, NetFeed flavor).

```json
{
    "bill_id": "bill_00042",
    "cycle": 14,
    "title": "The Public Safety and Sanitation Directive",
    "sponsor_id": "politician_3",
    "summary": "Authorizes Enforcer units to clear 'non-compliant' structures in designated urban zones, citing public health risk.",
    "stated_rationale": "To protect all citizens from the hazards of unregulated housing.",
    "honest_rationale": "Clear land in The Sinks for Enclave-adjacent logistics expansion.",
    "ideological_score": 0.7,
    "faction_preferences": {
        "CORPORATE_BLOC":  0.8,
        "POPULIST":       -0.9,
        "REFORM":         -0.6,
        "INDEPENDENT":     0.0
    },
    "proposed_effects": {
        "public_tension": 25,
        "security_presence": 15,
        "senate_alignment": 8,
        "region_modifiers": { "URBAN_SLUM": { "tension_modifier": 20 } }
    },
    "scandal_hooks": [
        "A leaked memo shows the sponsor's family trust holds 40% of the land being 'cleared'."
    ],
    "netfeed_flavor": "security-state framing; dissent cast as criminality"
}
```

- **`ideological_score`**: −1.0 (fully Pro-Sinks / redistributive) to +1.0 (fully Pro-Enclave / corporate). Drives every politician's fit calculation.
- **`honest_rationale`** is never surfaced to the player unless a `scandal_hook` is investigated and exposed. This is where the game's conspiracy layer lives.
- **`netfeed_flavor`** is a short string injected into the NetFeed's next prompt so coverage matches the bill's tone.

## Sponsor selection

Each cycle, `_pick_sponsor()` weights the 11 politicians:

```
weight = ambition
       + faction_pressure[faction](tension)
       + (re_election_proximity < 4) · populism · 2
       + (scandal_level > 50) · 0.5          # desperate moves by the already-falling
       = 0 if same politician sponsored last cycle
```

Faction pressure is *dynamic*. When `public_tension > 60`, Populists get a major bonus. When `senate_alignment > 70`, Corporate Bloc gets weighted up. A chamber under pressure produces bills from the faction feeling that pressure.

A cycle rarely passes without *someone* wanting to make a statement.

---

## LLM prompt shape

The heart of the system. The LLM is handed:

1. **World state snapshot** — all 5 globals + top 3 tension drivers from the last 3 cycles.
2. **Recent NetFeed** — last 5 headlines. Bills that ignore the news don't feel real.
3. **Active oligarch ambitions** — so a billionaire currently pursuing "Purge The Sinks" can plausibly have a Corporate Bloc ally sponsor exactly the bill they wanted.
4. **Sponsor profile** — Nature traits, faction, cause, scandal level, re-election proximity, behavioral profile, quirks (via `PoliticianData.get_llm_context_string()`).
5. **Forbidden topics** — titles of bills on the docket or passed in the last 2 cycles. No re-runs.
6. **Effect whitelist** — valid keys and ranges for `proposed_effects`.

The prompt then asks: *"One Bill this senator would sponsor **this week, in this world**, that fits their character and the political moment."* Explicit instruction: err toward *specific, concrete* framing over generic policy language.

**Fallback**: if the LLM times out or returns invalid JSON, `SenateDirector._fallback_bill()` produces a faction-appropriate placeholder. Minimal — the LLM is the default, fallback is offline safety.

## Voting resolution

Per-politician logic is in [politicians.md → voting loop](../03-characters/politicians.md#the-voting-loop-evaluate_bill). Senate-level tallying:

```
votes = []
for p in politicians sorted by charisma desc:
    stance = p.evaluate_bill(bill, world_state, peer_stances=votes)
    votes.append({politician_id: p.id, stance: stance})

yes     = count(stance == YES)
no      = count(stance == NO)
abstain = count(stance == ABSTAIN)

result = PASS if yes > no else FAIL
margin = yes − no                   # for NetFeed framing
```

`margin` drives headline tone. A 10–1 rout reads as *"Senate overwhelmingly adopts…"*; a 6–5 squeaker reads as *"Chamber fractured as…"*.

---

## Applying the outcome

On **PASS**, `WorldDirector._apply_bill_effects(bill)`:
- Adds each `proposed_effects.<var>` to the global economy (clamped to valid range)
- Applies `region_modifiers` to matching region shells
- Triggers NetFeed coverage with `netfeed_flavor` injected into the prompt
- Advances `oligarch_patronage`: YES-voters whose stance matched an oligarch's preference get +debt to that oligarch (they delivered)
- Updates each politician's `position_consistency` — flip-voters lose points; principled voters gain

On **FAIL**:
- Sponsor's `public_approval` shifts based on margin
- Sponsor's `scandal_level` rises if the bill had hooks and it failed ugly
- NetFeed covers the defeat; rival politicians get quotes
- No economy effects

## Player interaction window

Between bill proposal and vote tally, the player has **one world cycle** to act:

| Lever | Where | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Read the bill** | Senate docket UI | Reveals title, summary, sponsor, public `stated_rationale` |
| **Investigate** | Hack / interrogate / tail | Chance to surface `scandal_hooks` and `honest_rationale` |
| **Lobby a politician** | Per-politician actions | Bribe, blackmail, threaten, persuade (see [politicians.md](../03-characters/politicians.md#player-interactions)) |
| **Leak the bill** | NetFeed action | Public sees `honest_rationale`; Populists shift NO, `public_tension +10` |
| **Kill the sponsor** | Assassination | Bill drops from docket; +heat, +tension, faction replacement roll |
| **Do nothing** | — | The world legislates without you |

The cost of doing nothing *is* the feature: the Senate mutates the world whether or not the player engages. That is the political layer of the sim — it never pauses waiting for you.

## Example: one cycle

**Setup:** cycle 14. `public_tension = 72`, `senate_alignment = 38`, `food_price = 280`. The Food Oligarch is pursuing "Purge The Sinks." NetFeed's last three headlines covered slum unrest.

1. **Sponsor pick.** Corporate Bloc gets a pressure bonus (oligarch wants this). Populists also weighted up (they want to respond to tension). Roll lands on `politician_3`, a CORPORATE_BLOC senator with `ambition=0.8`, `populism=0.2`, `cause="law_and_order"`.

2. **Bill generation.** LLM returns:
   - Title: *"The Public Safety and Sanitation Directive"*
   - Summary: authorizes Enforcer clearance of non-compliant structures
   - Stated rationale: public health
   - Honest rationale: clears land for the Food Oligarch's logistics expansion
   - Effects: `public_tension +25`, `security +15`, `URBAN_SLUM tension_modifier +20`
   - Scandal hook: *"Sponsor's family trust holds 40% of the targeted land."*

3. **Player reads.** Options: leak it, bribe Independents, investigate the scandal hook, or ignore. Player investigates, finds the land trust doc, leaks it to NetFeed.

4. **Vote tally** (charisma order):
   - A Reform senator (`charisma=0.8`) votes **NO** first and pulls an Independent along.
   - Two Populists, already furious after the leak, vote **NO**.
   - The Bagman (bribed earlier cycle) votes **YES** regardless.
   - Two Corporate Bloc vote **YES**; one abstains after the scandal hit.
   - Final: **4 YES, 6 NO, 1 ABSTAIN. Bill fails.**

5. **Aftermath.** NetFeed: *"Senate rejects 'Safety Act' as land scandal engulfs sponsor."* `scandal_level` for politician_3 → 65. `public_tension −5` (small relief). The Food Oligarch's ambition stalls for a cycle; paranoia ticks up.

That is one cycle. Next cycle a new bill emerges — maybe an investigation *into* the sponsor, maybe a Populist counter-proposal, maybe something sideways nobody saw coming.

---

## Balancing

- **One bill per cycle, max.** Senate shouldn't drown the other systems. The player can ignore it and catch up.
- **Passed bills flavor NetFeed for 2 cycles.** Coverage stays relevant.
- **A sponsor cannot sponsor consecutively.** Forces faction rotation.
- **Assassination of a sponsor mid-cycle** kills the active bill *and* opens a vacant seat. High-risk play.
- **Patronage cap.** A politician can owe at most 3 oligarchs at once. Debt decays 10% per cycle — they're slowly becoming their own person again. Or they're being groomed.
