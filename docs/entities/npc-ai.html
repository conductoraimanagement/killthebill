NPC Persistent Characters & LLM Integration

NPCs are not disposable. At the start of each playthrough, the PopulationDirector generates a fixed roster of ~40 unique characters with randomized intrinsic personality traits. These NPCs persist for the entire run and evolve based on world pressure filtered through their immutable nature. No two NPCs react the same way to the same crisis.

Nature vs. Nurture Architecture

Every NPC's behavior is the product of two layers: Intrinsic Traits (who they fundamentally are) and Dynamic State (what the world has done to them). The same food crisis makes a high-aggression NPC radicalize into a Kaczynski type, while a high-conformity NPC becomes broken and submissive.

Intrinsic Traits (Nature — Immutable)

Set once at roster generation. These act as filters/multipliers on how world events affect the NPC's dynamic state.

Trait Range High Value Behavior Low Value Behavior
resilience 0.0–1.0 Stoic, absorbs pressure without cracking Fragile, breaks quickly under stress
aggression 0.0–1.0 Radicalizes when pushed, violent Withdraws, avoids conflict
empathy 0.0–1.0 Altruistic, affected by others' pain Self-serving, cold
idealism 0.0–1.0 Revolutionary, believes change is possible Cynical, resigned to the system
greed 0.0–1.0 Exploits chaos for personal gain Content, does not pursue wealth
conformity 0.0–1.0 Follows the crowd, hard to radicalize Independent thinker, lone wolf

Dynamic State (Nurture — Evolves Each Cycle)

STRESS Accumulated Pressure

stress_level (0–100). Rises when food prices spike, tension climbs, or security oppresses. Resilient NPCs absorb pressure slower. Drives behavior when combined with other dynamic variables.

HOPE Belief in a Future

hope (0–100). Decays under sustained oppression + poverty. Idealists lose hope slower. When hope drops below 30 and stress is high, the NPC is primed for radicalization or submission (depending on their nature).

RADICALIZATION Extremism Index

radicalization (0–100). Grows when stressed + hopeless, driven by aggression and braked by conformity. A high-aggression, low-conformity NPC under pressure becomes a Kaczynski. A high-conformity NPC under the same pressure just submits.

Derived Behavioral Profiles

The function get_behavioral_profile() evaluates Nature + Nurture and returns the NPC's current archetype. This is injected into the LLM prompt so it knows how to voice the character.

Profile Conditions
Radical Agitator Stress >70, Aggression >0.7, Conformity <0.3
Broken and Submissive Stress >60, Resilience <0.3, Conformity >0.6
Revolutionary Idealist Idealism >0.7, Empathy >0.6, Stress >40
Opportunistic Exploiter Greed >0.7, Empathy <0.3
Cautiously Stable Stress <30 or Resilience >0.7
Anxious Citizen Default fallback

LLM Context Serialization

get_llm_context_string() formats the NPC's full identity into a token-efficient prompt. It includes: name, class, behavioral profile, stress/hope/radicalization scores, personality adjectives (e.g., "aggressive, cynical, independent"), and the rolling memories array for narrative continuity.
