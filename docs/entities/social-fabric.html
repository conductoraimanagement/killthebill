Social Fabric Engine

The Social Fabric Engine simulates crowd psychology using an "Epidemic Influence Model." Because every NPC has persistent intrinsic traits (set at roster generation), the same world event produces vastly different crowd compositions depending on who is in the sector.

The Pressure Cycle

Every in-game cycle, the PopulationDirector.evolve_all_npcs() function passes the current global_economy state through every persistent NPC's process_world_pressure() method. Each NPC's intrinsic traits filter the same raw numbers into unique personal outcomes:

The Math: How Traits Filter Pressure

  • Stress: stress += pressure * (1.0 - resilience * 0.7) — Resilient NPCs absorb more before cracking.
  • Hope: hope -= decay * (1.0 - idealism * 0.6) — Idealists lose hope slower under oppression.
  • Radicalization: radical_push = aggression * 3.0 - conformity * 2.0 — Aggression drives extremism, conformity brakes it. Only fires when stressed AND hopeless.

Emergent Crowd Dynamics

Because each NPC processes the same world state through different personality filters, the crowd naturally stratifies without any scripted logic:

RADICALS High Aggression + Low Conformity

Under sustained pressure, these NPCs' radicalization spikes. They become Radical Agitators — Kaczynski types who reject both the system and the crowd. The player can recruit them for violent direct action, but they are unpredictable allies.

BROKEN Low Resilience + High Conformity

These NPCs crack first. Their stress overwhelms their capacity to resist, and their conformity means they submit rather than fight. They become Broken and Submissive — useless as allies but easy to exploit or ignore.

IDEALISTS High Idealism + High Empathy

Under moderate stress, these NPCs become Revolutionary Idealists. They believe in collective action and are the ideal candidates for organizing a political revolution (Victory Condition 3).

EXPLOITERS High Greed + Low Empathy

Regardless of stress, these NPCs are Opportunistic Exploiters. They thrive in chaos, hoarding resources and selling information. Useful as black market contacts but will betray the player for profit.

The Affinity System (Player Perception)

An NPC's opinion_of_player float (-1.0 to 1.0) is calculated using a multi-axis mechanism, now deeply influenced by intrinsic traits:

  1. Class Prejudice: Baseline modifier. Destitute NPCs distrust White Collar players. Enforcers distrust everyone below them.
  2. Economic Impact: If the player's actions hurt the NPC's immediate_need, affinity drops. High-empathy NPCs also lose affinity when others near them are hurt.
  3. Personality Contagion: High-conformity NPCs gradually adopt the crowd's opinion of the player. Low-conformity NPCs form their own judgment and may like the player precisely because the crowd hates them.
