Oligarchs & Billionaires

Each playthrough generates 4-6 unique billionaires with randomized personalities, ambitions, and quirks. They are not static targets — they are living characters who actively pursue their goals, react to the player, and reshape the economy every cycle through behavioral AI.

Design Philosophy

Oligarchs mirror the NPC Nature/Nurture model at the power level. They have immutable personality traits that filter world events into behavioral decisions. A ruthless Oligarch responds to riots with militia; a vain Oligarch responds with PR campaigns. Their ambitions create persistent, long-term systemic pressures that the player must navigate.

Ambitions

Each Oligarch is assigned 1-2 random ambitions at generation. These drive autonomous actions each cycle.

AGGRESSIVE Monopolize Supply

Acquires competitors, spikes prices in their sector. Costs 100k per action.

POLITICAL Achieve Political Immortality

Lobbies the Senate for permanent protections. Shifts senate_alignment +5.

VANITY Build a Legacy

Funds public works. Raises public_image but drains wealth heavily.

SURVIVAL Escape

Diverts funds to an offshore escape vessel. Only activates when paranoia >70.

MILITARY Crush the Resistance

Funds private militia to sweep The Sinks. Security +20, tension +15.

MEDIA Control the Narrative

Buys media outlets. Suppresses controversy by 20 points.

TECH Transcend Humanity

Funds augmentation programs. Tech prices skyrocket (+50).

EXTREME Purge The Sinks

Mass displacement. Tension +30, public image -20. Only for high ruthlessness.
