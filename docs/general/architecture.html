High-Level Architecture

KILL THE BILL is a 3D isometric immersive sim built in Godot 4. The core gameplay loop involves navigating a world rigidly divided by social class and manipulating a dynamic economy driven by a systemic "Butterfly Effect".

Core Pillars

Archetypes Player Characteristics

The player selects an archetype (White Collar / Blue Collar) and specific traits (like "Corporate Face"). These stats act as global modifiers that impact how the entire world reacts to them.

Exploration Sector Travel

The world is divided into distinct thematic sectors (The Sinks, The Spire). The player travels between these hubs, and each sector has its own evolving local dynamics.

Simulation Unscripted Living World

There are no pre-baked story paths. Victory is achieved systemically through Direct Action, Economic Collapse, or Political Revolution by pushing the simulation's boundaries.
