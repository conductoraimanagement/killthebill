# Core Loop

> Observe → Plan → Act → Adapt. Repeat until the Enclave falls or you're dust.

---

## 1. Observe
- **Read the NetFeed.** See what the world noticed.
- **Check the Oligarch summaries.** Who's paranoid, who's bleeding, who's ascending.
- **Talk to NPCs.** Their mood is the mass-level sentiment you can't read from a dashboard.
- **Travel.** Each region tells you something different about the same world.

## 2. Plan
- Identify targets, vulnerabilities, allies.
- Choose an approach: **stealth, persuasion, sabotage, violence**. Mix.
- If the map is ripe, a [cameo](../03-characters/cultural-cameos.md) may be offering something unusual. Decide whether to engage.

## 3. Act
- Execute. Every action fires the [Butterfly Effect](../02-world/butterfly-effect.md).
- Loud actions spike [Heat](heat.md). Silent actions don't — but the world may still move in response (see [NetFeed](../02-world/netfeed.md) silent ripples).

## 4. Adapt
- The world has changed. New opportunities. New threats.
- Allies may have radicalized, broken, died, or been arrested.
- Oligarchs may have shifted ambition or funded a militia.
- A cameo may have escalated mid-arc.

Back to Observe.

---

## A run-length perspective

A playthrough runs ~50–100 cycles depending on pace. Roughly:

| Cycle range | Phase | Feel |
|---|---|---|
| 1–5 | **Orient** | Learn who's who, which Oligarch owns what, where the Sinks' pressure points are |
| 5–20 | **Establish** | Build trust with 3–5 NPCs. First small sabotage. First scandal. |
| 20–50 | **Escalate** | Commit to a victory path or three. Heat rises. Cameos start appearing. Billionaires adapt. |
| 50+ | **Endgame** | Systemic collapse pressure. Revolution looms. The Enclave knows your name. |

These are not enforced bands. A ruthless player can compress all four into 20 cycles and die twice trying.

---

## Idle is not stopped

If the player "does nothing" for a cycle:
- Oligarchs still act
- The NetFeed still generates
- NPC radicalization still moves
- Cameos still roll their triggers
- The economy still drifts

There is no safe middle. The game penalizes caution as much as recklessness, just differently. This is on purpose — see [pillar 5](../01-vision/pillars.md#5-the-world-does-not-wait).
